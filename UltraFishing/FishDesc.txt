# Hedious

An affront to God Himself. A creation so vile that neither the soil below nor the heavens above will accept it, forcing it to be thrown around in the cold, uncaring winds between. It is believed that this creature was cast into this torment by its own creator, utterly disgusted of its hideous appearance.

It is, however, considered a delicacy among locals. Brave fishers risk their lives to catch this fish.

# tasty

A delicacy among machines. Due to its biological structure, it cannot survive outside its natural habitat for long. Highly sought after, there are many that cultivate this fish, but they are often in conflict with violent poachers.

Commonly found swimming along the roots of its source.

# electric

A highly energetic fish, it feeds off of any malicious data in its way. One of the smarter species, it will always take the shortest path to its destination.

Common in high-energy industrial environments.

# scrap

A machine in the form of a fish. Unlike its cousins, it seems to have settled for a lower, safer local optimum guaranteed by its environment, instead of engaging in high-risk combat. 

It reinforces itself using the mangled bits of scrap that litter its environment, and gathers nutrients from a constant supply of grinded flesh. Its form looks ugly to most, but beautiful to enthusiasts, which has led to many copycats.

# ancient

A manmade fish, created due to the extremely harsh environment in which it resides. Legends say that the text carved on it describes the construction of the monuments around it.

Prefers boiling hot water.

# wine

A valuable and luxurious fish. It is said that the older this fish becomes, the fancier it tastes, so it is often nurtured for prolonged periods of time before consumption.

A common sight in extravagant establishments and luxurious cruises.
